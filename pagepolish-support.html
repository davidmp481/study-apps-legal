PagePolish — Support

PagePolish turns photographed handwritten notes and diagrams into clear, editable pages on your iPhone or iPad. Scan or import a page, review the recognized words against the original, then restyle, rearrange, connect and export.

Common questions

Some words were read wrong. Open Review words & spelling to compare each label with a crop of your handwriting and pick a correction. For a specific area, use Read area to draw a rectangle on the original photo and reread just that part. Adding names and technical terms under Scan context & language before scanning improves recognition.

Two labels were merged, or one label was split. Select the boxes and use Combine into one or Split into labels. Both show a before/after preview and keep your connections.

Stray letters appeared from arrows or pen strokes. Open Review stray marks to set them aside or restore any that belong on the page.

How do I draw an arrow? Double-tap the first box, then double-tap the second. Choose straight, curved, right-angle or automatic connectors under Style.

How do I rearrange a diagram? Layout previews Bubble Map, Mind Map, Organization Chart, Cycle, Flowchart, Grid and Tidy arrangements. Original layout restores the saved positions. Undo reverses any applied change.

What does Cloud AI do, and what does it cost? Improve and Clean, Enhance diagram and Smart Rewrite are optional cloud actions that send the selected photo and text to the developer's server and OpenAI only when you confirm. Free includes 2 completed actions in total; PagePolish Pro includes 20 each month. Local scanning and editing never use cloud actions.

What is included in PagePolish Pro? Unlimited saved pages (Free keeps 3 at a time) and 20 cloud AI actions each month, billed monthly or yearly through your Apple Account. Pages you made stay editable and exportable if your subscription ends.

Restore a purchase. Tap the Free/Pro badge on the home screen, then Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Cancel a subscription. Manage it in your Apple Account's Subscriptions settings, or tap Manage subscription on the plan screen. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid renewal.
