Apex — Privacy Policy

Ski and snowboard session tracking for iPhone, by DMP Enterprise LLC.

Last updated: September 2026

1. The short version

Apex has no account, no sign-in, and no server. Everything it records stays on your iPhone. We do not collect, receive, sell, or share your data, and there are no analytics, advertising, or tracking components in the app.

2. What Apex records, on your device

When you start a session and grant permission, Apex records the following to your iPhone only:

  • Location. GPS positions, altitude, speed, and heading, about once per second, for as long as a session is running — including while your screen is locked. This is what produces your route, vertical, distance, and run and lift timings.
  • Motion and altitude. Accelerometer readings, used to look for possible jumps when park detection is on, and barometer readings, used to improve altitude tracking.
  • What you type in. Session names, notes, conditions, trick attempts, gear, trail notes, and goals.
  • Videos you choose to attach. If you attach a clip to a park attempt, a copy is stored in the app's own folder on your device.

Apex does not record audio, does not read your photo library, your contacts, your health data, or your other apps, and does not run in the background when no session is recording.

3. Where it is stored

Session data is written to a file in Apex's private Application Support folder, protected so it can only be read after you have unlocked your iPhone at least once since it was powered on. Attached videos are stored in the same private folder. No copy is sent anywhere.

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or to a computer, Apple's backup may include Apex's files, under Apple's terms rather than ours.

4. When anything leaves your device

Only when you choose to send it:

  • Maps. Turning on the map view for a session asks Apple Maps for map imagery. That request goes to Apple and is handled under Apple's privacy policy. The offline route sketch needs no connection and sends nothing. Apex uses no other network service.
  • Backups you export. "Prepare JSON backup" writes a file you can then share wherever you choose. It contains your recorded routes, which are precise records of where you were — treat it as private. Exported backups do not include attached videos.
  • Recaps you share. Sharing a session recap sends only the short text summary shown on screen.

5. Permissions you can decline

Location is required to record a session; without it the rest of the app still works. Motion is only requested when a session starts and only used for jump and altitude estimates — declining it turns those off and changes nothing else. You can change either at any time in iPhone Settings.

6. Deleting your data

Deleting a session in the app removes it and its jumps. Deleting a park attempt also deletes the clip attached to it. Deleting Apex from your iPhone removes everything the app stored, including recordings and attached videos. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side and no data-deletion request to make.

7. Purchases

Apex currently has no in-app purchases and no subscription. If that changes, purchases would be processed by Apple and we would still never see your payment details; this policy would be updated before any such feature ships.

8. Children

Apex is a general-audience app and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because it collects nothing, there is nothing for a parent or guardian to request or remove from us.

9. Not a safety device

Apex measures a phone, not a person. Its jump, landing, and run-quality figures are experimental estimates and none of them have been validated on snow. Nothing in the app is a safety rating, a coaching judgment, or a substitute for your own decisions on the mountain. Lift and queue times are timings of your own past recordings, not live resort wait times.
