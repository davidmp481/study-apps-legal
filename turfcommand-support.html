Frequently Asked Questions

How does the subscription work?

TurfCommand Pro is $3.99/month with a 1-week free trial. The subscription is billed through your Apple Account and renews automatically until cancelled. You can manage or cancel it any time in Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions on your device — cancelling during the trial means you won't be charged.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Make sure you're signed into the same Apple Account, then open TurfCommand and tap Restore Purchases on the subscription screen.

Where is my data stored? Does it sync?

Your data lives on your device and in your private iCloud account. If you're signed into iCloud, your customers, schedules, and invoices sync automatically across your own devices. The developer has no server and cannot see your data — see the Privacy Policy.

Why does the app ask for my location?

Location is only used for the optional crew clock-in/out feature, to record where a visit happened. You can decline the permission and use everything else normally.

I found a bug — what should I send?

Email us with your device model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened (screenshots help). We'll get it fixed.
