Frequently Asked Questions

Can this app tell me what is wrong with my car or appliance?

No. Sound Diagnosis measures a recording and shows provisional troubleshooting checks drawn from published sources. It cannot confirm a failed component, certify that equipment is safe to operate, or establish that a repair worked. Its real-world diagnostic accuracy on vehicles and appliances has not been established. Treat what it shows as a starting point for a conversation with a qualified professional, not a verdict.

What are the "sound clues"?

They are an experimental, optional feature that runs Apple's general-purpose on-device sound classifier. It recognizes broad everyday sound categories, not specific mechanical faults. It can be wrong, it does not identify a failed part, and it never changes your own description of the problem.

Why does the app want microphone access?

Only to record the sound you are asking about, and only when you start a recording. If you decline, you can still import an audio file, browse the troubleshooting guide, and listen to the bundled examples.

Where do my recordings go? Do they get uploaded?

They stay in the app's private storage on your iPhone. The app makes no network requests, has no account, and never uploads your audio or uses it for training. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I get a good recording?

Record while the sound is actually happening, get reasonably close to the source, and keep the recording steady for several seconds. Avoid talking over it and avoid wind. The app reports recording-quality measurements so you can tell when a take is too quiet, too short, or clipped — if it flags a problem, record again before reading anything into the results.

Can I import a recording I already have?

Yes. Import a WAV, MP3 or M4A file through the file picker. Very short, empty, or extremely long files are rejected.

How do I compare a sound before and after a repair?

Save the original as a case, then add a new recording to that same case after the work is done and note the reported outcome. The app keeps them side by side. The comparison shows how the sound changed; it does not prove that the repair worked.

How do I share a case with a mechanic or technician?

Open the case and use the export action. You choose which fields and which audio to include, and where to save it. Remember that an export can contain your notes and any voices captured in the recording.

How do I delete my data?

Remove an individual case to delete its saved audio, or open About & privacy and choose Erase all local data to remove everything. Deleting the app also removes its data.

Is there a subscription?

No. Sound Diagnosis has no in-app purchases and no subscription.

I found a bug — what should I send?

Email us with your iPhone model, iOS version, the app version from About & privacy, and a short description of what happened (screenshots help). We'll get it fixed.
