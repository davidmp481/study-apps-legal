Aircraft Design Lab — Support

Mac App Store · Apple Silicon · macOS 12 or later

Aircraft Design Lab lets you design model-scale aircraft, fly them in a simulated landscape, run repeatable flight tests and compare what changed. This page covers the most common questions. For anything else, email davidmpeterson1998@gmail.com and include the app version from Aircraft Design Lab ▸ About.

Getting started

  • Choose Start here ▸ Learn to fly for a guided trainer flight.
  • Flight keys: Space start / pause, R and F throttle, A and D bank, W and S pitch.
  • In Build, drag a wing or engine to move it; hold Shift while dragging for height.
  • Templates holds 21 editable concepts: planes, gliders and multirotors.

Free trial and Full Unlock

Building and flying are free. The flight test lab, the Compare view and saving, opening or exporting project files are included in a free 14-day trial and then unlocked permanently with the one-time Full Unlock purchase. Start either from the badge in the header or from Aircraft Design Lab ▸ Unlock Full Version…. The trial runs 14 days from the day you start it, follows your Apple Account, and does not renew or charge anything when it ends.

Already purchased on another Mac, or reinstalled? Choose Aircraft Design Lab ▸ Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple Account.

Saving your work

The current project saves automatically on this Mac. Use File ▸ Save Project… to write a portable project file, and File ▸ Open Project… to bring one back. Compare results can be exported as CSV.

Joysticks and gamepads

Connect a compatible USB or Bluetooth controller before launching the app, then open the joystick setup from the Fly view to map axes and set sensitivity. Keyboard flight is always available.

Performance

If frame rates drop, switch the graphics preset to Balanced or Performance in the flight setup. The app requires an Apple Silicon Mac.

Something went wrong

  • If the workbench stops responding, choose View ▸ Reload Workbench. The latest autosave is restored.
  • If a purchase does not unlock, wait a moment and choose Restore Purchases.
  • Still stuck? Email the address above with your macOS version and what you were doing.

Privacy

The app collects no personal information. See the Aircraft Design Lab privacy policy.

Limits

Aircraft Design Lab simulates model-scale concepts. Complete aircraft performance is unvalidated, and the app is not intended for real aircraft engineering approval, flight training certification or flight safety decisions.
