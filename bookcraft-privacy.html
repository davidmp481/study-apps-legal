Bookcraft — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Bookcraft: Book Formatter turns a manuscript (a Word document, PDF or text file) into a typeset book on your iPhone, iPad or Mac. Everything happens on your device. The developer collects no data from the app.

1. Your manuscript never leaves your device

Files you import, the books you create, your designs, saved styles and any fonts you add are stored only on your device, in Bookcraft's own storage. Typesetting and exporting (PDF, EPUB and Word) are performed on the device. Nothing is uploaded to the developer or to any server, and the app makes no network requests to read, store or process your writing.

2. No accounts, no analytics, no ads

Bookcraft has no sign-in, no analytics SDKs, no advertising and no third-party tracking. The optional one-time unlock is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store; the developer never sees your payment details.

3. Sharing your exports

When you export a book, the file is handed to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes next (Files, Mail, AirDrop, a publishing service) is your choice and is governed by that destination's own policy.

4. Deleting your data

Deleting a book in the app removes it from your device. Deleting the app removes all books, styles and fonts. Because nothing is stored anywhere else, there is nothing further to delete.

5. Children

Bookcraft is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
