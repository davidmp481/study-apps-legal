Sound Diagnosis — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Sound Diagnosis is an iPhone app for recording an unfamiliar piece of equipment, measuring the recording, keeping notes and repair records, and comparing a sound before and after a repair. This policy describes how that app handles information. It applies to Sound Diagnosis only.

1. What We Collect

Nothing is collected by the developer. Sound Diagnosis has no account, no login, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs. We do not operate a server that receives your data. The app makes no network requests of its own.

2. Microphone and Recordings

The app asks for microphone access the first time you start a recording, not at launch. If you decline, you can still use every other part of the app, including importing an audio file and browsing the troubleshooting guide.

Audio you record is written to the app's own private storage on your iPhone. It is not uploaded, not sent to the developer, and not used to train any model.

3. What Is Stored on Your Device

The following stays in the app's private storage on your iPhone:

  • Audio you record, and audio files you choose to import;
  • Equipment details, operating context and notes you type;
  • Saved cases, follow-up answers and reported repair outcomes;
  • Measurements calculated from your recordings.
  • Recording setup, microphone and phone model information, baseline selections, and optional background measurements;
  • Technician-review drafts, reviewer details and permission-document references you choose to enter. These stay unverified and are not sent automatically.

Saved audio and case files are marked for exclusion from device backup. Deleting the app removes these files. Removing a case inside the app deletes its saved audio.

To avoid comparing recordings from different phones or microphones as if their setup matched, the app stores a random local installation token and a microphone fingerprint. A device-only, non-synchronizing Keychain token helps detect restored or migrated files. These are not advertising identifiers, are never uploaded, and are always removed from review exports, even when recording details are included. The Keychain token can remain after uninstalling the app; a reinstall creates a new comparison identity. Erase all local data in About & privacy removes the app's saved records, audio and these local comparison tokens.

4. Analysis Happens on Your iPhone

Waveform, frequency and recording-quality measurements are calculated on your device. The optional, experimental sound clues use Apple's general-purpose on-device sound classifier. No audio leaves your iPhone for analysis.

5. Importing and Exporting Files

When you import audio, you pick the file yourself through the iOS file picker and the app reads only that file. When you export a case, you choose which fields and which audio to include, and you choose where iOS saves it.

Please note that an export you create can contain equipment identifiers, timestamps, your notes, and any voices or background sound captured in a recording. If you save an export to a location that syncs to a cloud service, that copy is handled by that service under your own account and settings. Choose the destination and the recipient with that in mind.

6. Bundled Example Recordings

The app includes six example recordings published under CC0 or in the public domain. Each one is credited in the app with its author, original source and license. They are played locally and involve no network request.

7. Links Out of the App

The app links to technical reference sources and to this policy. Opening one hands the address to your browser, and the destination site's own terms apply.

8. Purchases

Sound Diagnosis has no in-app purchases and no subscription.

9. Children

Sound Diagnosis is a maintenance and repair tool for a general adult audience and is not directed at children. Because the app collects no personal information, none is collected from users of any age.

10. What This App Does Not Do

Sound Diagnosis measures sound and applies provisional, source-linked troubleshooting rules. It cannot confirm a failed component, certify that equipment is safe to operate, or establish that a repair worked. Have a qualified professional verify a cause before acting on it.
