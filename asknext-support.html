AskNext — Support

AskNext is an interview copilot: it records and transcribes on your device, tracks your question guide, suggests the next thing to ask, and writes the summary afterwards.

Common questions

Cards aren't appearing. AI features run on your device with Apple Intelligence. They need an iPhone 15 Pro or later (or an iPad with M1 or later) with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings, and AI switched on for the interview. Recording and transcription work on every device, online or offline.

The other person's voice is faint. Put the phone on the table between you and don't use AirPods or a headset: iOS routes the microphone to them.

A speaker label is wrong. Tap the speaker name on any line in the Transcript tab to flip it. During the interview, the "I'm speaking" button labels your own speech.

Restore a subscription. Settings → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple account.

Cancel a subscription. Open Settings on your iPhone → your name → Subscriptions → AskNext Pro.
