Aircraft Design Lab — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Aircraft Design Lab is a Mac app for designing model-scale aircraft concepts, flying them in a simulated landscape, running repeatable flight tests and comparing the results. This policy describes how the app handles information. It applies to Aircraft Design Lab only and is published by DMP Enterprise LLC.

1. What We Collect

Nothing is collected by the developer. Aircraft Design Lab has no account, no login, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking SDKs. We do not operate a server that receives your data, and the app does not send your designs, flight recordings, controller input or usage information to us.

2. What Is Stored on Your Mac

The following stays in the app's own sandboxed storage on your Mac:

  • Your aircraft projects, named versions and the comparison baseline;
  • Recorded flights and lab test results;
  • Preferences such as units, graphics quality, lighting, sound and joystick settings.

Deleting the app removes this storage. Nothing in it is uploaded.

3. Opening and Saving Files

When you open a project you choose the file yourself in the macOS open panel, and the app reads only that file. When you save or export, you choose where macOS writes the file. If you save to a location that syncs to a cloud service, that copy is handled by the service under your own account and settings.

4. Game Controllers

If you connect a compatible joystick or gamepad, the app reads its axes and buttons locally to fly the simulated aircraft. Controller input is never recorded for the developer or transmitted anywhere.

5. Purchases

Aircraft Design Lab is free to download. Building and flying are free. The flight test lab, the Compare view and project file saving are included in a free 14-day trial and then unlocked permanently with a one-time in-app purchase called Full Unlock. Both the trial and the unlock are App Store products processed entirely by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy. The app checks your purchase status with the App Store through Apple's StoreKit framework; the developer does not receive your name, Apple Account or payment details. Restore Purchases is available in the app menu.

6. Network Use

All simulation resources are bundled with the app and work offline. The app's only network use is Apple's StoreKit framework, which communicates with the App Store to load product prices and confirm purchases.

7. Bundled Reference Data

The app includes airfoil and propeller measurements from the University of Illinois (UIUC) databases and an attributed NTNU flight-reference data subset. These are read locally. Their sources and terms are credited inside the app.

8. Links Out of the App

The app links to this policy, a support page and Apple's standard license agreement. Opening one hands the address to your browser, and the destination site's own terms apply.

9. Children

Aircraft Design Lab is a design and simulation tool for a general audience and is not directed at children. Because the app collects no personal information, none is collected from users of any age.

10. What This App Does Not Do

Aircraft Design Lab simulates model-scale concepts. Complete aircraft performance is unvalidated, and the app is not intended for real aircraft engineering approval, flight training certification or flight safety decisions.
