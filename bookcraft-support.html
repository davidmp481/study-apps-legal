Bookcraft — Support

Bookcraft: Book Formatter turns a Word document, PDF or text file into a typeset, print-ready book on your iPhone, iPad or Mac, with full control over the design, and exports it as a print PDF, an EPUB ebook or a Word file.

Common questions

My chapters weren't found correctly. Open the book → Chapters. Swipe right on a chapter to merge it with the next one, or open a chapter and swipe right on a paragraph to start a new chapter there. Using Word's Heading 1 style, or lines like “Chapter One”, gives the best automatic detection.

My PDF imported as one long chapter. PDFs carry less structure than Word files. Split it in Chapters, or import the original .docx if you have it. Scanned PDFs need OCR first — Bookcraft reads text, not images.

Which trim size and paper should I choose on Amazon KDP? The same trim size you chose in Design → Page, and the paper colour shown on the Export screen. Upload the Print PDF as your interior.

Can I use my own font? Yes: Design → Type → Import a Font (.ttf or .otf). Check that the font's licence allows embedding in books you sell.

The export has a watermark. Free exports are full books with a “Bookcraft Preview” watermark. The one-time unlock removes it and adds EPUB and Word export for every book.

Restore a purchase. Settings → Restore Purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account.
