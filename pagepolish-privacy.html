PagePolish — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

PagePolish turns photographed handwritten notes and diagrams into editable pages on your iPhone or iPad. Scanning, editing and exporting happen on your device. The only time anything leaves your device is when you explicitly ask for an optional Cloud AI action, and this policy explains exactly what is sent and kept when you do.

1. Your pages stay on your device

Photos you scan or import, the pages you create, your scan context and vocabulary, and your edits are stored only on your device in PagePolish's own storage. Text recognition uses Apple's on-device Vision framework. Local editing, styles, layouts, fonts and exports (PDF, PNG, SVG and text) make no network requests and send nothing to the developer.

2. Optional Cloud AI

PagePolish offers optional cloud actions: cleaning up a scanned diagram, correcting labels, and Smart Rewrite for notes. A cloud action runs only when you choose it and confirm the upload. When you do, the app sends the selected photo (resized and re-encoded without location or camera metadata), the recognized text and its positions, the current diagram or note, and any scan context you entered, through the developer's server to OpenAI, which generates the result.

  • The developer's server does not store your photos, text or diagrams. It forwards the request and returns the result.
  • OpenAI processes the request under its API terms. Requests are sent with storage disabled, but OpenAI's own retention and abuse-monitoring policies apply. See OpenAI's data controls.
  • Simply opening the app, the editor or the plan screen sends no page content anywhere.

3. Purchases and the AI allowance

PagePolish Pro is an auto-renewable subscription sold through Apple. Apple processes the payment; the developer never receives your card details or Apple Account email. To provide the included AI allowance and prevent abuse, the app sends Apple's signed app and subscription information to the developer's server with each cloud action and allowance check. The server keeps a pseudonymous record derived from that Apple identity together with:

  • the current plan and billing period,
  • how many AI actions were used in the period and whether each succeeded,
  • the AI model used, token counts and an estimated processing cost.

This record contains no page content, photos, names or contact details. It is retained for as long as needed to operate the allowance and for up to 24 months afterwards for accounting and abuse prevention, then deleted. Because the free allowance is tied to the Apple app identity, deleting and reinstalling the app does not reset it.

4. No accounts, no analytics, no ads

PagePolish has no sign-in, no analytics SDKs, no advertising and no third-party tracking. It does not use your data to train AI models.

5. Permissions

The camera is used only to scan pages when you tap Scan a page. The photo library is accessed only through the system picker for photos you choose. You can decline either and still use the rest of the app.

6. Sharing your exports

Exported files are handed to the iOS share sheet. Where they go next is your choice and is governed by that destination's own policy. Device backups may include your pages.

7. Deleting your data

Deleting a page in the app removes it and its source photo from your device. Deleting the app removes all local data. To request deletion of the server-side usage record, email the address below from the device and we will remove it.

8. Children

PagePolish is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
