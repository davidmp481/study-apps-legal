AskNext — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

AskNext is an interview copilot for iPhone and iPad. It records an interview, transcribes it, suggests follow-up questions and writes a summary, all on your device. The developer collects no data from the app.

1. Audio never leaves your device

Recordings are captured and stored only on your device, in AskNext's own storage. Transcription is performed on-device using Apple's speech recognition framework. The developer does not receive, store, or have access to your audio.

2. The AI runs on your device too

AskNext's AI features (live suggestions, question guides, summaries and notes) run on your device using Apple Intelligence through Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework. Your transcript, question guide and any context you type are processed on the device and are not sent to the developer or to any server. On devices without Apple Intelligence, AI features are unavailable and nothing changes about where your data lives.

3. No accounts, no analytics, no ads

AskNext has no sign-in, no analytics SDKs, no advertising, and no third-party tracking. Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store; the developer never sees your payment details.

4. Your responsibilities when recording

Recording laws vary by location, and many places require every participant's consent. AskNext shows a consent reminder before each recording, but you are responsible for obtaining consent and complying with the laws that apply to you.

5. Deleting your data

Deleting an interview in the app removes its audio, transcript and documents from your device. Deleting the app removes everything. Because nothing is stored anywhere else, there is nothing further to delete.

6. Children

AskNext is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
