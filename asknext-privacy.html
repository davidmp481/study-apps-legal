AskNext — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

AskNext is an interview copilot for iPhone and iPad. It records an interview, transcribes it on your device, and can use an AI service to suggest follow-up questions and write a summary. This policy explains exactly what leaves your device and what does not.

1. Audio never leaves your device

Recordings are captured and stored only on your device, in AskNext's own storage. Transcription is performed on-device using Apple's speech recognition framework. The developer does not receive, store, or have access to your audio.

2. What is sent to the AI service, and when

When AI features are on for an interview, AskNext sends short text excerpts of the transcript, your question guide, and any context you typed (for example a job description) to a server operated by the developer, which forwards them to Anthropic's Claude API to generate suggestions and summaries. This happens only:

  • during an interview with AI turned on (small rolling windows of recent text),
  • when you ask for a summary or document after an interview, and
  • when you ask AskNext to generate interview questions.

The developer's server stores nothing: it holds each request only for the seconds needed to process it and keeps no copies of transcripts, names, or context. Anthropic processes API requests under its commercial terms, which do not permit training on API data. Turn AI off for an interview and nothing is sent anywhere; recording, transcription, highlights and export still work.

3. No accounts, no analytics, no ads

AskNext has no sign-in, no analytics SDKs, no advertising, and no third-party tracking. Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store; the developer never sees your payment details.

4. Your responsibilities when recording

Recording laws vary by location, and many places require every participant's consent. AskNext shows a consent reminder before each recording, but you are responsible for obtaining consent and complying with the laws that apply to you.

5. Deleting your data

Deleting an interview in the app removes its audio, transcript and documents from your device. Deleting the app removes everything. Because nothing is stored on our servers, there is nothing further to delete.

6. Children

AskNext is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
